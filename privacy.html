Privacy Policy

Updated October 29, 2026

Loupe doesn’t collect, store, or share your data.

Pages you inspect

Loupe reads a page only when you open Loupe on it. Everything stays on your Mac and is cleared when you close Loupe, leave the page, or close the tab.

Network

Loupe makes no network requests. There are no accounts, analytics, ads, or tracking.

Settings

Your settings, such as color format and shortcuts, are saved on your Mac. They contain no page data.

Clipboard

Values you copy go to your clipboard. With Universal Clipboard, they can sync to your other Apple devices.

Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in macOS settings, Apple may send us crash reports. They don’t include page data.

Changes

Updates to this policy will be posted here.
