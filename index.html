Design inspector for Safari.

Get started

  1. Open Loupe and choose Open Safari Settings.
  2. Turn on Loupe in Extensions.
  3. On any website, click the Loupe button in the toolbar.

Shortcuts

  • Click to pin an element
  • Arrow keys to move between elements
  • Hold Option to measure
  • F to freeze hover, C to copy CSS
  • Esc to exit
  • Option-Shift-L to open Loupe

Loupe doesn’t open on a page

Safari doesn’t allow extensions on its own pages, the App Store, or PDFs. On other sites, choose Allow when Safari asks.
